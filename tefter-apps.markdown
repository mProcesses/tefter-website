<section class="section">
  <div class="wrapper">
    <h2 class="section-title">TX First, Apps When You Need Them</h2>
    <p class="section-copy">
      TX is the exchange layer that keeps data moving between organizations — ERP to ERP, system to system — through mismatches, outages, and format differences. Tefter Apps are optional operational surfaces that provide ready solutions for daily business operations.
    </p>
    <div class="two-column" style="margin-top: 30px;">
      <div class="column">
        <h3>TX alone</h3>
        <ul style="list-style-type: disc; padding-left: 20px; line-height: 1.8;">
          <li>Connect your ERP to partner ERPs reliably</li>
          <li>Handle format mismatches and mapping exceptions</li>
          <li>Resume automatically after network outages</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
      <div class="column">
        <h3>TX + Apps</h3>
        <ul style="list-style-type: disc; padding-left: 20px; line-height: 1.8;">
          <li>Add mobile capability where your ERP falls short</li>
          <li>Give field teams low-click workflows</li>
          <li>Run standalone or integrate with your ERP</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
