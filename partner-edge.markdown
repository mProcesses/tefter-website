<section class="section">
  <div class="wrapper section-center">
    <h1 class="section-title">Partner Edge — Your B2B Portal</h1>
    <p class="section-copy">
      Partner Edge is a branded B2B portal where your partners place orders directly — like a private webshop for your business relationships. Partners log in, browse your catalog, and submit orders. No EDI setup. No integration projects. Orders flow through TX into your systems automatically.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <div class="wrapper">
    <h2 class="section-title">Why Partner Edge</h2>
    <p class="section-copy">
      In B2B, not every partner can connect ERP-to-ERP. Some send orders by email. Others call. Some use spreadsheets. Partner Edge brings all of them into one consistent flow — under your brand.
    </p>
    <div class="card-grid">
      <div class="card">
        <h3>Private webshop for partners</h3>
        <p>Partners get a familiar e-commerce experience — browse products, add to cart, place orders — but it's your catalog, your pricing, your domain.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <h3>Orders flow through TX</h3>
        <p>Every order submitted through Partner Edge flows through Tefter Exchange — same reliability, same auditability, same recovery as your ERP-connected partners.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <h3>Instant partner onboarding</h3>
        <p>No integration project. No EDI setup. Send a login, and your partner is transacting the same day.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
