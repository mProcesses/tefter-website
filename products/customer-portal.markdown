    <h2 class="section-title">Under the Hood: Powered by TX</h2>
    <p class="section-copy">Every order placed through the Customer Portal flows through <strong>Tefter Exchange (TX)</strong>—our stateful execution engine that guarantees delivery even when networks fail.</p>
    <div class="card-grid">
      <div class="card">
        <h3>Automatic Retry</h3>
        <p>If your ERP is temporarily down, TX queues the order and retries automatically. No orders lost. No manual intervention.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <h3>Transaction Recovery</h3>
        <p>If data doesn't match (SKU mismatch, pricing error), TX pauses the order, routes it for approval, then resumes—without duplication.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <h3>Full Audit Trail</h3>
        <p>Every order has complete history: who placed it, when, what happened, who approved changes.</p>
