<section class="section">
  <div class="wrapper">
    <h2 class="section-title">The Challenge</h2>
    <p class="section-copy">In 2022, MMM Lasta was a growing wholesale distributor managing 50+ customer relationships through a patchwork of email, phone calls, and manual spreadsheets. Order processing was time-consuming and error-prone:</p>
    <ul class="feature-list">
      <li>Sales reps took orders by phone and manually entered them into the system</li>
      <li>Email orders required manual transcription, leading to frequent errors</li>
      <li>No visibility into order status for customers or internal team</li>
      <li>Warehouse operations relied on paper pick lists</li>
      <li>Scaling required proportional increases in administrative staff</li>
    </ul>
    <p class="section-copy">The manual processes were limiting MMM Lasta's ability to grow. Every new customer added operational complexity and overhead.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <div class="wrapper">
    <h2 class="section-title">The Solution</h2>
    <p class="section-copy">MMM Lasta implemented Tefter's complete platform in early 2022:</p>
    <div class="card-grid">
      <div class="card">
        <h3>1. Customer Portal</h3>
        <p>Small and medium customers were onboarded to a branded B2B portal where they could browse the catalog and place orders directly.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <h3>2. Mobile Operations</h3>
        <p>Sales reps received mobile apps for order taking on the go. Warehouse staff transitioned from paper to smartphone-based picking and packing.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <h3>3. Integration Engine (TX)</h3>
        <p>For major customers with EDI requirements, Tefter's TX engine handled format mismatches and network failures automatically.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

</section>
