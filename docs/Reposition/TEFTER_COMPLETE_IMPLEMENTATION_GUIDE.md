### MMM LASTA CASE STUDY PAGE (/customers/mmm-lasta)

**HERO**
**Headline**: How MMM Lasta Grew 112% in 2 Years with Tefter
**Subtitle**: Wholesale distributor eliminates manual order processing, scales from €4.3M to €9.2M revenue

**Meta**: Industry: Wholesale Distribution | Location: Belgrade, Serbia | Customer Since: 2022 | Employees: 35 (from 20)

---

**METRICS HEADLINE**

+112% Revenue Growth
€4.3M → €9.2M (2022-2024)

75% Team Expansion
20 → 35 employees

€271K Net Profit (2024)
Healthy 2.9% margin

---

**THE CHALLENGE**

In 2022, MMM Lasta was a growing wholesale distributor managing 50+ customer relationships through a patchwork of email, phone calls, and manual spreadsheets. Order processing was time-consuming and error-prone:

- Sales reps took orders by phone and manually entered them into the system
- Email orders required manual transcription, leading to frequent errors
- No visibility into order status for customers or internal team
- Warehouse operations relied on paper pick lists
- Scaling required proportional increases in administrative staff

The manual processes were limiting MMM Lasta's ability to grow. Every new customer added operational complexity and overhead.

---

**THE SOLUTION**

MMM Lasta implemented Tefter's complete platform in early 2022:

**1. Customer Portal**
Small and medium customers were onboarded to a branded B2B portal where they could browse the catalog and place orders directly. This eliminated email and phone order chaos for the majority of customers.

**2. Mobile Operations**
Sales reps received mobile apps for order taking on the go. Warehouse staff transitioned from paper to smartphone-based picking and packing, improving accuracy and speed.

**3. Integration Engine (TX)**
For major customers with EDI requirements, Tefter's TX engine handled format mismatches and network failures automatically, reducing IT support burden.

---

**THE RESULTS**

**Revenue Growth**
MMM Lasta grew from €4.3 million in revenue (2022) to €9.2 million (2024), a 112% increase in just two years. The operational efficiency enabled by Tefter allowed them to take on more customers without proportional increases in administrative overhead.

**Team Expansion**
The company grew from 20 to 35 employees (75% increase). Critically, this growth was concentrated in revenue-generating roles (sales, logistics) rather than administrative support, thanks to automation of order processing.

**Profitability**
Despite rapid growth, MMM Lasta maintained healthy profitability, achieving €271,000 in net profit in 2024 (2.9% margin). The operational efficiency gains from Tefter contributed directly to bottom-line performance.

**Operational Efficiency**
- Manual order entry eliminated for 80%+ of customer orders
- Zero IT tickets for customer order issues
- Real-time visibility into inventory and order status
- Customer satisfaction improved due to faster, more accurate order processing
